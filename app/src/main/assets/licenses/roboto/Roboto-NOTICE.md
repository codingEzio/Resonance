# Roboto combo notices

These browser assets contain the Google Fonts families Roboto, Roboto Condensed,
and Roboto Mono. The source TTFs are pinned to Google Fonts commit
`809e4d8b8d7e9364a914909bb777679606c178b8`; the WOFF2 files are generated from
those TTFs with `pyftsubset`, preserving the variable axes and upstream glyph
set.

All three families are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Keep the
matching `OFL-Roboto*.txt` files when redistributing them. The families cover
Latin and related scripts, not CJK. Chinese uses the platform CJK fallback in
the Roboto mode.
