# Compact font notice clarifications

The retained producer NOTICE describes Cubic 11 as 1.500. The accompanying
`provenance.json` instead records embedded version 1.451 at revision
`4c566f7d6cc5c05ee360fe9cff56b5da1fcafd4d`, with SHA-256
`0193f5f033612496df6b45ee92ac3b335bc6a5a24ff95da55ca87b33e57dcf62`.
This correction applies to that exact input. The pinned Cubic license and
inherited notices remain in `licenses/Cubic11.txt`.

The producer NOTICE describes Font Logos as Unlicense, while the retained
`licenses/NerdFontsAudit.txt` describes it as Unlicensed. The [Font Logos 1.3.0
license](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lukas-w/font-logos/v1.3.0/LICENSE)
and [Nerd Fonts 3.5.1 glyph summary](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ryanoasis/nerd-fonts/v3.5.1/src/glyphs/README.md)
retain these differing descriptions. The Nerd Fonts 3.5.1 [import script](https://github.com/ryanoasis/nerd-fonts/blob/b894ea7803af6aade63d60a4381e006098ec9c4d/bin/scripts/lib/i_logos.sh)
labels its Font Logos input 1.4.0, while the glyph summary lists 1.3.0. The
[1.4.0 license](https://github.com/lukas-w/font-logos/blob/v1.4.0/LICENSE)
is also Unlicense. These conflicting source records do not establish the source
or license status of each glyph in the bundled font. Preserve both notices. The Font Logos
[README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lukas-w/font-logos/v1.3.0/README.md)
separately reserves trademark owners' rights. These sources do not establish
clearance for every glyph or grant trademark rights; review remains open.
